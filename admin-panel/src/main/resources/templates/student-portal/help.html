Frequently Asked Questions

Open the New Complaint page, select the relevant department and category, enter the complaint details, review the information and submit it.

Open My Complaints and select a complaint to view its details and status. The current portal uses sample complaint data.

If you are in immediate danger in India, call 112 directly. The current portal SOS button is a demo and does not send an emergency alert.

Open Lost & Found and use Report an Item to preview the report form. The current form is for demonstration and does not save or publish reports.

The current My Profile page is read-only. Profile editing and database integration can be implemented later.

Check that the application is running, verify the URL and internet connection, and contact your institution's authorized technical support team if the issue continues.
